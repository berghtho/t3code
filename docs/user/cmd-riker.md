# CMD Riker

You can keep writing and send another message while Riker responds. Sending a follow-up redirects
the Lead Agent to your latest message.

Choose **Interrupt** to stop the Lead's current response. Worker Sessions keep running, and your
unsent draft stays in the composer. You can also send `/interrupt` as a message.

The conversation opens at the latest message and follows new replies while you are near the bottom.
Scrolling up keeps your reading position. Return to the bottom or send a message to follow replies
again.

Sending and interrupting require permission to operate the project and a connected Owner Gateway.
